# Smart Sweep — manual for the City Corporation (City Viewer)

**Who this is for:** officers of a City Corporation or Pourashova who oversee the
waste collection service and the agencies that run it.

**What you can do:** read everything about service quality in your city —
collection, complaints, agencies, collectors, vans and targets. You **cannot**
change records, and you do **not** see any agency's private money (bills,
payments, cash, dues).

**Your screens:** City Dashboard · Live Map · My Profile.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open Smart Sweep and choose **Staff**.
2. Enter your mobile number and tap **Send code**; enter the 4-digit SMS code.
3. Next time, sign in with your number and the **PIN** you set in *My Profile*.

You land on the **City Dashboard**. If your account is set to one city, you see
only that city. If not, a **city picker** at the top lets you choose one; every
tab then shows that city.

---

## 2. Your daily routine (5 minutes)

1. **Summary** tab — read *Picked up today* and *Complaints open / overdue*.
2. **Collection** tab — check which wards have *missed* pickups.
3. **Complaints** tab — open *Overdue complaints* and follow up with the agency.
4. Once a month: **Targets** and the **Summary league table** for the agency review meeting.

---

## 3. The City Dashboard, tab by tab

| Tab | What it shows | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| **Summary** (সামারি) | Holdings served this month, target achievement, picked up today, open complaints, collectors checked in, vans in service, the **agency league table** and the **KPI scorecard** | Get the whole picture in one screen |
| **Collection** (কালেকশন) | Today: due, confirmed, still pending. For the period: collected %, confirmed by agency %, auto at cut-off, missed. A daily chart and a table **by ward** | See where pickup fails |
| **Service trend** (সার্ভিস ট্রেন্ড) | Holdings registered and served per month or year, by agency, supervisor, collector or ward — with *new*, *dropped* and an up/down trend | See whether coverage grows or shrinks |
| **Complaints** | Complaint types and how they change, performance per agency (received, resolved, within deadline %, average hours, reopened), and every **overdue** complaint | Hold agencies to their deadlines |
| **Registers** | Read-only lists: agencies, collectors, supervisors, vans & drivers, documents expiring in 60 days | Check licences, fitness and contracts |
| **Targets** | Each agency's target holdings against holdings served, with good / fair / poor status | Review contracts monthly |

### How "collected" is counted

| On the day | Result |
|---|---|
| The agency scanned or confirmed the holding | **Collected (confirmed)** |
| No confirmation and no complaint by the cut-off | **Collected (auto)** |
| A resident reported "waste not collected" for that day | **Missed** |

A high *confirmed* share means the agency proves its work. A high *auto* share
means it relies on nobody complaining.

### Exporting

Every table has **CSV / Excel / PDF** buttons. The export is made from the same
figures you see on screen.

---

## 4. Live Map

Where the vans are right now (from their GPS units) and a pin for every located
holding.

---

## 5. If something goes wrong

| You see | It means | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Collection history is empty | No collection day has been closed yet | Ask the system administrator to check the day-end job |
| A tab says it could not load | No connection to the server | Tap **Retry** |
| You see another city's name in the picker | Your account is not tied to one city | Ask the super admin to set your city |
| You need money figures | They are the agency's private records | Ask the agency for its monthly report pack |
