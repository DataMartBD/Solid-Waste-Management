# Smart Sweep — manual for the Collector

**Who this is for:** field staff who collect waste door to door, using a phone.

**What you see:** **your own round** — the buildings on your routes — and the
holdings and complaints of your ward.

**Your screens:** Daily Collection (your main screen) · Routes · Holding Master ·
Surveys · Complaints · Live Map · Daily Dashboard · My Profile.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open Smart Sweep on your phone and choose **Staff**.
2. **First time:** enter your mobile number → **Send code** → enter the SMS code.
3. Go to **My Profile → Set PIN** and choose a 4-digit PIN.
4. **From then on:** number + PIN. This works even when the signal is weak.

Forgot your PIN, or locked after 5 wrong tries? Sign in with an SMS code instead.

---

## 2. Your day

1. **Check in** with your supervisor (they mark your attendance).
2. Open **Daily Collection** — today's stops in walking order.
3. At each building, **scan its QR tag**.
4. If you cannot collect, **record a skip** with the reason.
5. When back in signal, check nothing is left in **Queued to sync**.

---

## 3. Daily Collection

### Recording a collection

1. Tap **Scan QR code**.
2. Hold the building's tag inside the frame.
3. Done — one entry for the whole building.

No tag, or the camera will not focus? Find the stop in the list and tap
**Collect**.

### What a scan tells you

| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Collected | It is on your round | Go to the next stop |
| **Already collected** | Recorded earlier today | Nothing |
| **Not on your round** | It belongs to another route | Do not collect; tell your supervisor |
| **Unknown tag** | Not registered, or damaged | Tell the office |
| **Could not read** | The camera saw nothing | Clean the tag, scan again |

### Recording a skip

Tap the stop → **Skip** → choose a reason:
No one home · No waste to collect · Premises locked · Could not access ·
Householder refused.

A skip with a reason is **not** counted against you as a miss. The stop stays in
**Pending** so you can come back. Collecting it later replaces the skip.

### No signal?

Keep working. Everything is saved on the phone under **Queued to sync** and
uploads by itself when the signal returns. One bad entry never stops the rest.

---

## 4. Other things you may do

- **Payments** — money a household gives you is recorded against your name with
  the time. Hand it in to the office promptly; the office can see how long cash
  has been with you.
- **Surveys** — fill the door-to-door form; it works offline too.
- **Holdings** — verify a building's location while standing at its door.
- **Complaints** — act on tickets in your ward: advance, add a note, attach a
  photo, resolve.

---

## 5. How you are measured

Each month the **collector scorecard** looks at:

- **Coverage** — how many of your buildings you collected from.
- **On time** — finishing within the route's time window.
- **Few complaints** — complaints about your buildings.
- **Bill collection** — money collected against bills.

Scans are your proof of work, so **scan every stop**.

---

## 6. If something goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| Wrong PIN, account locked | Sign in with SMS code |
| A building is not in your list | Ask your supervisor to add it to your route |
| Your queue does not upload | Keep working; it uploads when the signal returns |
| You are signed out | Sign in again — queued entries are still on the phone |
