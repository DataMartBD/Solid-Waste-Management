# Smart Sweep — manual for the Supervisor

**Who this is for:** supervisors who run the daily work in a set of wards.

**What you see:** only **your wards** — their holdings, routes, collectors,
complaints, collections and payments.

**Your screens:** Daily Dashboard · Live Map · Holding Master · Surveys · Daily
Collection · Routes · Route Plan · Complaints · Billing · Fleet & Vans ·
Collectors · Operations Reports · Finance Reports · Users · My Profile.

---

## 1. Your daily routine

**Morning**
1. **Collectors** — mark each collector **checked in** or **absent**.
2. **Route Plan** — read the *Still to do* strip; give every route a collector.
3. **Operations Reports → Collection → Daily collection → By collector** —
   yesterday's due, scanned, skipped and missed.

**During the day**
4. **Complaints** — assign new ones; watch the deadline clock.
5. **Live Map** — where the vans are.

**End of day**
6. **Operations Reports → Missed & skipped** — plan return visits.

---

## 2. Collectors

### Marking attendance

**Collectors** → a collector → **Checked in** or **Absent**. Do it every morning:
at the start of each day everyone is reset to absent until marked in. A
collector who scans a stop also counts as present that day.

### Adding a collector account

**Users → Add user** → role **Collector** (the only role you can add) → name,
mobile number and wards (only your own wards are offered) → Save.

### Giving a collector a route

**Collectors** → route icon on the row (or **Assign route** in the details panel).
Route Plan opens on the collector with the route picker. Pick a route. **Back to
Collectors** returns you.

---

## 3. Routes (Route Plan)

### The "Still to do" strip

| It says | Click it to |
|---|---|
| N routes without a collector | List just those routes |
| N routes with no stops | List just those routes |
| N holdings not on a route | Open Holding Master filtered to them |

### Creating a route

1. **New route** → name, ward, collection window → optional **Collector**.
2. **Create route** — the stop picker opens; tick holdings and add them.
3. Use the arrows to put stops in walking order, or **Sort** for holding order.

### Changing a route's collector

Open the route → **Walked by → Change** → pick someone. Off-route collectors
cannot be picked. Taking a route from another collector asks you first.
**Remove** leaves the route with no collector.

### Rules

- A holding is on **one** route only; adding it elsewhere moves it.
- Only **verified** holdings can be routed, and only to a route of their ward.

---

## 4. Holdings

- **Register holding** — building, owner, units, service charge, families.
- **Verify the location** at the door so it can be routed.
- On a holding, the **Route & collector** panel adds it to a route or moves it.
- On the list, filter **Not on a route**, tick holdings and **Add selected to route**.

---

## 5. Complaints

1. New tickets arrive with a priority and a deadline:

| Priority | Must be resolved within |
|---|---|
| Urgent | 4 hours |
| High | 12 hours |
| Medium | 24 hours |
| Low | 48 hours |

2. **Assign** the ticket to a collector of the ward.
3. **Advance** it (Assigned → In progress), **Add note**, **Attach photos**.
4. **Resolve** with how it was fixed. A resident can have it **Reopened**.

Every action is recorded with your name. A "waste not collected" complaint marks
that day as **missed** for the agency.

---

## 6. Reports you will use

| Report | Where | What for |
|---|---|---|
| Daily collection | Operations → Collection | Due vs scanned / skipped / missed, by day or collector |
| Missed & skipped | Operations → Collection | Which holdings were not served, how often, why |
| Route completion | Operations → Collection | Stops scanned and whether the route finished on time |
| Attendance | Operations → Staff | Present, absent, late, per collector or per day |
| Repeat complaints | Operations → Complaints | Holdings complaining 2+ times in 30 days |
| Household dues | Finance | Who owes, and for how long |

---

## 7. If something goes wrong

| You see | It means | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| A collector cannot be picked for a route | They are off route | Bring them back on route first |
| A holding will not go on a route | Not verified, or another ward | Verify it; use a route of its ward |
| "No collector assigned" on a route | Nobody walks it | Assign one |
| A complaint for yesterday is refused | It is past the deadline (noon) | Log it for today if it applies |
