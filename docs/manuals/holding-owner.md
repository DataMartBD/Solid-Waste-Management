# Smart Sweep — manual for Holding Owners and Residents

**Who this is for:** owners of a registered building (holding) and the families
living in it.

**What you can do:** see whether your waste was collected, make a complaint and
follow it, and manage your PIN.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open Smart Sweep and choose **Holding owner / Resident**.
2. Enter your **mobile number** (the one registered with your holding).
3. Enter your **PIN**. The first PIN is the **last 4 digits of your mobile number**.
4. You will be asked to **change it** — please do, because anyone who knows your
   number could guess the first one.

"This number is not registered"? Contact your agency or ward office to have your
number added to your holding.

After 5 wrong PINs, wait 15 minutes and try again.

---

## 2. Daily Collection

Shows each of your holdings and today's result:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Collected (confirmed)** | The collector scanned your building's tag |
| **Collected** | Recorded as collected at the day's end |
| **Not collected** | A "waste not collected" complaint was made for that day |
| **Skipped** | The collector recorded a reason (e.g. premises locked) |
| **Not yet** | Not collected so far today |

---

## 3. Making a complaint

1. Open **Complaints → Make a complaint**.
2. Choose **What is the problem?** — Waste not collected · Bin overflowing · Bill
   problem · Staff behaviour · Other.
3. For **Waste not collected**, choose **Today** or **Yesterday**. Yesterday can
   be reported until the deadline shown (usually 12:00 noon today).
4. Add details and up to **3 photos** if you like.
5. Tap **Send complaint**. You get a complaint number.

### What happens next

- Every complaint has a **deadline** the agency must meet.
- You can follow it on the **Complaints** tab: Open → Assigned → In progress →
  Resolved.
- A "waste not collected" complaint marks that day as **missed** for the agency,
  and the City Corporation sees it.

**Owners** see every complaint about their building. **Residents** (tenants) see
their own.

---

## 4. My Profile

- Your name and your holdings.
- **Change PIN** — enter your current PIN and the new one twice. Simple patterns
  (1111, 1234) and the last 4 digits of your number are not allowed.

---

## 5. Help

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Number not registered | Contact your agency or ward office |
| Forgot your PIN | Contact your agency or ward office to reset it |
| "Too late to report yesterday" | The deadline has passed; report today if it applies |
| Cannot reach the server | Check your internet connection and try again |
