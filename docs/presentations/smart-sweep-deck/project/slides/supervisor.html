Benefits · Supervisor

Route Plan & daily sheet

Run your wards from one screen

Know each morning who is out, what is left, and what needs a visit.

  • Build routes and assign collectors in one step
  • To-do list: routes with no collector, holdings on no route
  • Daily sheet per collector: due, scanned, skipped, missed
  • Attendance by day, with late arrivals marked
  • Complaints in your wards, assigned and closed on time
  • Holdings that are missed or complain again and again
The supervisor is the person who keeps the day running. On Route Plan they build a route, pick its collector and add its holdings in one flow, and a to-do strip shows what is still missing: routes with nobody assigned, routes with no stops, and verified holdings not on any route. Each morning the daily sheet shows, per collector, what was due and what was scanned, skipped or missed. Attendance is kept day by day, with late arrivals flagged against the route’s start time. Complaints from their wards come to them with a deadline. And two follow-up lists — missed and skipped holdings, and holdings that complain repeatedly — tell them exactly where to go. A supervisor only ever sees their own wards.
