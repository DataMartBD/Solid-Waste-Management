Operations & Finance Reports

Prove your work, control your money

Everything an agency needs to run and grow its contract.

  • QR scans prove every pickup — fewer disputes with the City
  • Collector scorecard: coverage, on-time, complaints, bill collection
  • Cash in hand: who holds collected money, and for how many days
  • Dues ageing by holding, so staff know exactly whom to call
  • Fuel and repair cost per van, with vans that need a check
  • Monthly report pack as a PDF, ready to submit to the City
For an agency, Smart Sweep turns daily work into proof. Every QR scan is evidence that the holding was served, so disputes with the City are settled by data. The collector scorecard ranks collectors each month on coverage, on-time completion, complaints and bill collection — a fair basis for rewards. On the money side, “cash in hand” shows how much each collector has collected but not yet deposited, and for how long; dues ageing lists every unpaid holding by how late it is, with the owner’s phone number. Fleet reports show fuel cost per kilometre and per holding, flag vans burning more fuel than usual, and track repairs. And at month end, one click produces a PDF report pack to submit to the City. The agency also sees “what the City sees” — its own rank and target — so there are no surprises.
