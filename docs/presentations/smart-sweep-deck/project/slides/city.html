City Dashboard

See every agency, every day

Real figures to manage contracts, not reports written by the contractor.

  • Daily pickup: confirmed, auto-collected and missed, by agency and ward
  • Agency league table and target achievement each month
  • Complaints answered within deadline, and every overdue one
  • Service growth: holdings served, gained and lost, month by month
  • Registers of agencies, collectors, vans and expiring papers
  • One-click export (Excel, CSV, PDF) for meetings and audits
For the City Corporation the main benefit is visibility. The City Dashboard shows, every day, how many holdings were due, how many the agency proved with a QR scan, how many were assumed collected at the cut-off, and how many were missed because a resident complained — split by agency and ward. Each month agencies are ranked in a league table and measured against the target holdings in their contract. Complaint performance shows how many were answered within the deadline and lists every overdue one. The service trend shows whether coverage is growing or shrinking. Registers list every agency, collector and van, with licences and fitness papers about to expire. Everything exports to Excel or PDF. Note: the City Viewer sees service and complaints, but never an agency’s private finances.
