Today’s round, on the phone

Clear work, fair credit

A simple screen built for the street, not the office.

  • Today’s stops in walking order, with the time window
  • Scan the QR at the door — the scan is proof of the work
  • Works with weak signal: 4-digit PIN, scans sync later
  • Skip with a reason (locked, nobody home) — not blamed for it
  • Every taka taken is recorded, so nobody can dispute it
  • A fair monthly score that can be used for rewards
For the collector we kept the screen very simple. Each morning they see today’s stops in walking order and the time window. At each door they scan the holding’s QR — and that scan is their proof: nobody can later claim they did not come. Many lanes have poor signal, so collectors can sign in with a 4-digit PIN, and scans are kept on the phone and synced when the signal returns. If a stop cannot be served — locked, nobody home, refused — they record the reason, and it is not counted against them as a miss. Money they take is recorded against them with the time, so there is never an argument about cash. And the monthly scorecard gives an honest, transparent basis for rewards.
