How it works

From a building to a report, in six steps

1

Holding

Registered, QR-tagged, location verified

2

Route

Holdings grouped by road, with a time window

3

Collector

Assigned to routes, checks in each day

4

Pickup

QR scan at the door; day closes at cut-off

5

Bill & complaint

Monthly bill; complaints tracked to a deadline

6

Report

Daily, monthly and contract reports

Every step is recorded once and reused everywhere — so the City, the agency and the resident see the same facts.

Here is the whole flow. One: a holding is registered and its location is confirmed by someone standing at the door — only verified holdings can be planned. Two: holdings are put on routes, one route per road, each with a collection time window. Three: a collector is assigned to routes and checks in every morning. Four: at each stop the collector scans the QR; at the city’s cut-off the day closes, and a resident’s missed-collection complaint marks that holding as missed. Five: bills are generated monthly, and complaints are tracked against a deadline. Six: all of this feeds the reports. The key point: each fact is entered once, at the source, and everyone reads the same record.
