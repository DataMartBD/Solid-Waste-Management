Benefits · Holding owner & residents

Citizen portal

A service you can see — and a voice that counts

Sign in with a mobile number. No office visit, no phone queue.

  • See whether your waste was collected today
  • Report a missed collection or other problem in a minute
  • Follow your complaint until it is resolved
  • Every complaint has a deadline the agency must meet
  • A missed-pickup report counts against the agency’s record
  • One clear monthly charge per holding, recorded with each payment
For residents, the change is simple: the service becomes visible, and their complaint actually counts. They sign in to the citizen portal with their mobile number and a PIN. They can see whether their holding was collected today. If it was not, they report it in a minute, and follow the complaint until it is resolved. Every complaint carries a deadline the agency has to meet, and the City sees how many were answered on time. Importantly, a missed-collection report changes the record: that day is counted as missed for the agency, so residents directly influence how agencies are judged. The service charge is one clear monthly amount per holding, and every payment is recorded. Owners see every complaint on their building; tenants see their own.
