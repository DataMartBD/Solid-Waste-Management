The solution

One system, built around the holding

Every building gets a record, a QR tag, a route and a collector. Every pickup, bill, payment and complaint is tied to it.

Register

Holdings, households and GPS location, verified on site.

Collect

Routes, daily QR scans and an automatic cut-off for the day.

Bill & pay

Monthly bills, payments, deposits and remittance to the City.

Report

Dashboards and reports for every level, in Bangla and English.

Smart Sweep puts the holding — the building — at the centre. Each holding is registered with its owner, households and a verified GPS location, and gets a QR tag. Holdings are grouped into routes, and routes are assigned to collectors. Every day the collector scans the QR at each stop; at the city’s cut-off time the day closes automatically, and anything a resident did not complain about counts as collected. Bills are raised monthly per holding, payments are recorded with who took the money, and the money is followed from the collector to the agency to the City. On top of all that, every user gets reports for their own level, in Bangla or English.
