Today’s challenge

Waste collection runs on paper, trust and guesswork

No proof of service

Nobody can show which holding was served today, and which was missed.

Cash goes missing

Collected money passes through many hands, with no clear record of who holds it.

Complaints get lost

Residents call, nothing is tracked, and the same houses complain again and again.

The City sees nothing

The City Corporation cannot measure its contractors or plan with real figures.

Let us start with what most cities face today. First, there is no proof of service: a contractor says the road was covered, a resident says nobody came, and nobody can prove either. Second, money: service charges are collected in cash, often by the collector at the door, and there is no record of how long that cash stays in someone’s pocket. Third, complaints are phone calls or paper registers; they are not tracked to a deadline, so the same holdings complain again and again. And finally, the City Corporation — who pays for and is responsible for the service — has no numbers to measure its agencies or to plan. Smart Sweep was built to close exactly these four gaps.
