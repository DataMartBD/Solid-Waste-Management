A few words on control and trust, because this matters to both the City and the agencies. Access is by area: a supervisor only ever sees their own wards, an agency only its own data, and the City Viewer only their city. The City Viewer sees service quality and complaints, but never an agency’s private money. Every figure is backed by evidence: QR scans, holdings whose location was verified on site, and dated records. Several agencies can work in one city, and when a collector moves to another agency their history moves correctly. The whole system works in Bangla and English, and one installation can serve several cities.
