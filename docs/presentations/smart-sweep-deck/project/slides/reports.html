Plus a Daily Dashboard for today’s work. Every table exports to Excel, CSV or PDF.

All reports sit on three pages, each answering one question. The City Dashboard answers: how are the agencies doing for the city? Operations Reports answer: is the waste being collected, by whom, and with what? Finance Reports answer: were customers billed, did they pay, and where is the money? Day to day, staff start on the Daily Dashboard for today’s figures. Every table can be downloaded as Excel, CSV or PDF, and the agency’s monthly report pack is one PDF. If time allows, this is a good moment for a short live demo of the City Dashboard and the collector scorecard.
