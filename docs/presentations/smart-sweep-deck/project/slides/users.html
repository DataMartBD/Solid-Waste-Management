Who uses it

Five kinds of user, each with their own screen

City Corporation

Oversees every agency in the city

Agency

Runs the service under contract

Supervisor

Manages wards, routes and complaints

Collector

Collects door to door, on a phone

Holding owner

Residents who receive the service

Super admin

Sets up cities, agencies and users

Smart Sweep has five kinds of user, plus the system administrator. The City Corporation oversees the service. Agencies — private contractors, NGOs or the city’s own in-house team — run it. Supervisors manage a set of wards. Collectors do the work door to door. And holding owners and residents receive the service. Each one signs in with a mobile number and sees only their own area: a supervisor sees only their wards, an agency only its own data, and the City Viewer sees the whole city but no agency’s private money.
